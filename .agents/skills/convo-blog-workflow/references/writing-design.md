# Writing and article design

## Reader-first strategy

Answer one real question for a named reader. Lead with the useful answer, then qualification and next steps. Match the search intent, but do not claim a query has demand unless measured. Select an angle because the tenant can support it with specific knowledge, not because a competitor used the same headings.

## Prose

- Natural Australian spelling by default; adapt only to an explicit tenant/market locale.
- Concrete subjects and verbs; explain jargon. Vary sentence/paragraph length naturally.
- No generic “in today's fast-paced world”, “unlock”, “delve”, “game-changer”, repeated summary paragraphs or empty superlatives.
- No fake first-person experience, customer anecdotes, quoted advice, statistics, credentials, awards or guaranteed outcomes.
- Never alter factual meaning to make copy sound less machine-written.
- Discuss costs, timing and limitations only to the level supported by evidence. Avoid false precision.
- Keywords appear where they clarify the subject; never force variants into every heading or repeat locations unnaturally.
- No fixed word count padding. Scope follows question complexity and evidence.

## Canonical article

Required: title, sensible slug, concise honest meta description, answer-first introduction, logical sections and relevant next action. Stable IDs for editable blocks. Checkable claims reference source evidence outside public-facing prose when appropriate.

Optional blocks only when useful: FAQ answering distinct questions; callout for a real caution; comparison table with genuinely comparable supported facts; steps for a real sequence; checklist for action; quote/proof only with source and publication permission; licensed/tenant-owned image with meaningful alt text. Text-first layout is a valid complete article.

Do not force a quote, table, FAQ or image into every post. Schema suggestions reflect visible supported content; no fabricated ratings, authors or FAQ answers. Do not expose run IDs, provider names or prompt jargon in the article itself.

## Visual review

One H1; hierarchical H2/H3 sections; readable measure and line spacing; adequate contrast; lists and callouts with semantic HTML; narrow-screen tables scroll without widening the page. CTA is clear and restrained, not repeated after every section. No giant empty hero when an image is absent.

Preview and HTML/Markdown export share the canonical article model. WordPress theme appearance must be inspected separately before claiming matching presentation.

## Links

Article hyperlinks must follow the tenant's approved internal host/path policy. Evidence URLs may include external research sources in private artifacts; that does not make them allowed in public output. Reject unsafe schemes, credentials-in-URL and host tricks. Check image/media sources under their own approved asset policy; never silently loosen article link rules.
